At Top 5, Exa Highlights returned direct answers in 86.1% of scheduled slots (1,722 of 2,000). Keenable Pro returned direct answers in 78.6%, and Perplexity Web in 73.3%.

Each row counts the first 3, 5 or 10 positions for every question, including empty positions. An answer supports the question from the returned evidence. A lead points to useful evidence without establishing an answer. An unresolved slot has no settled grade. Other slots include results graded neither and empty positions.
